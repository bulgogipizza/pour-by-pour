    Hi, thanks for stopping by.

    What this is. A map of my co-op as a materials testing technician at EXL Engineering in Surrey, BC. Each stop is one concrete placement I tested in the field: slump, air content, temperature, and test cylinders for strength.

    Why I made it. "Tested 100+ placements" is hard to picture on a résumé. I wanted to show where the work happened across Metro Vancouver and how it grew, from lab-heavy weeks in May to site work on most days by September.

    What it's built from. 106 concrete placement records I filled out between May 11 and Oct 1, 2026: date, site, structural element and pour volume. Site names are replaced with numbers and locations are shown at neighbourhood level.

    How to use it. Press play to replay the season, or drag the slider to jump to any pour. The cylinder and milestones fill up as the volume adds up.
